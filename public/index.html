Getting started

What is this site for?

It's our band's shared planner. Everyone marks when they're free, and the site finds the best times for all of us to practice. It also keeps our practices, shows and show-day schedules in one place.

How do I get in?

Open the link, type the band passcode, then pick your name (or type it the first time). Your phone or computer remembers you after that.

I'm on a new phone or computer. Will my dates be there?

Yes. Enter the passcode and tap your same name from the list. Your dates are saved under your name, not your device. Picking a different spelling creates a new person.

How do I switch to a different person?

Tap switch next to your name at the top right, then pick the right name.

Marking when you're free

How do I mark my availability?
  1. Go to Availability and make sure My times is selected.
  2. Tap a day: once for Free (green), twice for Maybe (yellow), three times for Busy (red), four times to clear it.
  3. That's it. It saves by itself. You'll see Saved near the top.
Can I say I'm only free at certain times?

Yes. After you mark a day Free or Maybe, use the quick buttons under What times? (like After 6 PM or Until 5 PM), or pick exact From and Until times. Leave them on All day if you're free the whole day.

What's the difference between Free and Maybe?

Free means you can definitely make it. Maybe means you might. Maybes still count when finding a time, but Free counts more, so the best times favor people who are sure.

How do I change or remove an answer?

Tap the day again to cycle through the options, or tap it and use the Free / Maybe / Busy / Clear buttons in the panel.

Copying your week

My schedule is the same most weeks. Do I have to fill in every day?

No. Fill in one normal week, then:

  • Copy from last week… copies last week into the week you're looking at.
  • Repeat for 4 weeks… copies the week you're looking at into the next 4 weeks.

Both open a list where you tick only the days you want. It shows what each day will get, and warns you if it will replace something you already marked.

How do I copy just one day?

Tap the day and use ↻ Same as last Monday (or whatever day it is) in the panel.

I copied by mistake. Can I undo it?

Yes. Tap Undo last copy in the week bar right after copying.

How do I pick which week I'm working on?

Use the ‹ › arrows in the week bar. It says This week, Next week and so on, with the dates.

Finding a time that works

How do I see when everyone's free?

Tap Everyone on the Availability tab. The box at the top shows the best time coming up. The list on the right shows each day's best time for the next 60 days, with the most people first.

What do I see when I tap a day?

Best rehearsal times for that day: the top time windows, who can make each one and who's missing. Below that, a small chart shows each person's free hours, with the best window outlined.

It says nobody overlaps. What does that mean?

People are free that day, but at different times (for example, one person 5–7 PM and another 7–11 PM). Ask people to fill in more days or wider times.

Practices, shows and itineraries

How do I plan a practice?

Tap Plan practice next to a good time. The date and time are filled in for you. Add where and what you're working on, then save. You can also use + Plan practice on the Schedule tab.

How do I add a show?

On the Schedule tab, tap + Add show. Add the venue, date, and any times you know (load-in, soundcheck, doors, set). You can fill in more later with Edit.

What's the Itinerary tab?

A timeline for each show day. It combines the venue times with your own steps, like "Meet at rehearsal space" or "Dinner". Add steps with Edit schedule. Copy for group text copies the whole day so you can paste it into the band chat.

How do I delete something?

Tap Delete, then tap it again to confirm. That stops accidental deletes.

What does the warning on someone's name mean?

Their free time doesn't cover the whole plan, for example they're free after 7 PM but load-in is at 6 PM. Tap or hover over their name to see why.

What the colors mean

  • Green: free
  • Yellow: maybe
  • Red: busy
  • Purple dot: a show that day
  • Blue dot: a practice that day
  • Green outline: everyone's free and their times overlap
  • Blue underline: the week you're copying

Privacy and problems

Who can see this?

Only people with the band passcode. It isn't listed on Google. Anyone with the passcode can view and edit, so only share it with the band.

My changes aren't showing up for others.

Look for Saved near the top after you change something. Other people's screens update within a few seconds. If you see a yellow "Can't reach the server" note, check your internet and refresh the page.

There's a duplicate or old person in the band list.

Go to Availability → Everyone, scroll to Band, tap Remove next to the name, then tap it again to confirm.
